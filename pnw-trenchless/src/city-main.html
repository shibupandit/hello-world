Sewer and water line problems we see in Spokane Valley

Many older Spokane-area homes still run on clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines and galvanized water service. Add mature pines and maples, and root intrusion is one of the most common problems we see.

  • Roots in the line: backups that keep coming back after the line is cleared.
  • Cracked or collapsed pipe: old clay and Orangeburg lines past their life.
  • Low water pressure: often a corroded galvanized water service.
  • One bad spot: a single crack or joint on an otherwise good line.

How a trenchless repair works

  1. Camera inspection. We record the full line and show you the footage.
  2. Written quote. The method that fits your pipe, and the price, before any work.
  3. The repair. Lining, bursting, a new water line or a point repair, through one or two small access points.
  4. Final camera run. Proof the line is fixed. You keep the before and after footage.

Trenchless or digging?

With a traditional dig you also pay to restore everything that came out of the ground. Trenchless repair leaves your lawn, driveway and trees in place in most cases, and your water or sewer is off for hours, not days.
