Quick answer

What is pipe bursting?

Pipe bursting is a trenchless way to replace a sewer line. A cone-shaped bursting head is pulled through the old pipe from one access pit to another. It breaks the old pipe outward into the soil, and a new, seamless HDPE pipe follows behind it in the same path. You end up with a completely new pipe, and it can be a size larger than the old one.

Is bursting right for your pipe?

When bursting works, and when it doesn't.

Bursting replaces the whole run between two pits, so it shines when the old pipe is past saving. The camera shows which fix fits, and we'll show you the footage either way.

Good candidates

  • Collapsed or crushed sections
  • Orangeburg (fiber pipe) that's deformed
  • Clay pipe that's broken or badly offset
  • Pipe too small for the household (upsize)
  • Long runs under lawns, driveways and landscaping

Usually a different fix

  • Pipe still round, with cracks or rootsPipe lining
  • Line under a slab or inside the housePipe lining
  • Bellies or sags holding waterSpot excavation
  • One damaged spot on a good linePoint repair
How pipe bursting works

The bursting process, step by step.

From the first camera run to the final one, here's what happens under your yard.

  1. Camera inspection

    We record the full line, measure its length and depth, and mark every defect and connection.

  2. Locate & dig two pits

    Utilities are located, then we dig one small pit at each end of the run. Typical pit size.

  3. Fuse the new pipe

    Lengths of HDPE are heat-fused into one continuous, joint-free pipe, sized for your line or upsized.

  4. Burst & pull

    The bursting head is pulled through the old line, static / pneumatic, breaking it outward as the new pipe follows.

  5. Reconnect

    The new pipe is connected at the house and at the main or septic, and any branch lines reopened.

  6. Verify & restore

    A final camera run proves the result, then the pits are backfilled and the site is cleaned up. You get the footage.

How long does an HDPE pipe last?

HDPE pipe has an expected lifespan of 50+ years, and its heat-fused joints leave nothing for roots to get into. PNW warranty terms.

Can you make my pipe bigger?

Often, yes. Because the old pipe is broken outward, bursting can pull in a larger pipe than the one you have. Typical upsizing limits.

How much digging is there?

Two small access pits, one at each end of the run, instead of a trench along the whole line. Typical pit size and depth.

Can I use my water and toilets during the job?

How long the line is out of service during bursting and reconnection.

Is it safe for nearby gas, water and other lines?

All utilities are located and marked before we dig, and the camera and locator show where your line runs. Mike to describe his clearance checks.

Is pipe bursting or pipe lining better?

It depends on the pipe. Lining needs a host pipe that still holds its shape. For collapsed or deformed pipe, or to upsize, bursting pulls in a brand-new HDPE pipe.
