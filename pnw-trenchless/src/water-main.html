Quick answer

What is water line pulling?

Water line pulling is a trenchless way to replace the water service line that feeds your home. Instead of digging a trench the whole length of the line, we open small access pits and pull a new CTS poly line underground. Then we reconnect it and test it for pressure and leaks. The new line doesn't rust or build up scale, so you get your water pressure back.

Is a new water line right for you?

When a new line fixes it, and when it won't.

A new service line fixes problems that start in the line itself. If the trouble is somewhere else, we'll tell you before anything is dug.

Good candidates

  • Old galvanized service line
  • Rusty or discolored water at the tap
  • Low water pressure through the whole house
  • Repeated leaks or a wet spot in the yard
  • Lines under lawns, driveways and landscaping

Usually a different fix

  • Low pressure at one faucet onlyA plumber
  • Leak on the city side of the meterYour water utility
  • Slow drains, backups or sewer smellSewer lining
  • Collapsed or broken sewer pipePipe bursting
How water line pulling works

The water line pull, step by step.

From finding the line to the pressure test, here's what happens under your yard.

  1. Find the line

    We find where your service line runs, from the meter to the house, and check what it's made of and why it's failing.

  2. Locate & dig small pits

    Utilities are located, then we dig small access pits instead of a trench. Where the pits go and typical size.

  3. Pull the new line

    A new CTS poly line is pulled underground along the path, method Mike uses.

  4. Reconnect

    The new line is connected at the meter and at the house, fittings and shutoff details.

  5. Pressure & leak test

    We test the new line for pressure and leaks before the job is done.

  6. Restore

    The pits are backfilled and the site is cleaned up. Your water is off for hours, not days.

How long will my water be off?

Hours, not days. The old line keeps working while the new one goes in, and we tell you exactly when the water goes off for the reconnection. Typical shutoff time.

What is CTS poly pipe?

It's polyethylene water pipe made to copper tube size (CTS), so it connects to standard fittings. It doesn't rust or build up scale inside like old galvanized pipe does.

Will a new line fix my low water pressure?

If the pressure is lost in a corroded or leaking service line, yes. If the cause is the city main or your house plumbing, a new line won't fix it, and we'll tell you before you spend the money.

How much digging is there?
