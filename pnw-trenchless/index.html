The basics

What is trenchless pipe repair?

Instead of digging a trench the full length of your sewer or water line, we open one or two small access points and repair or replace the pipe underground. Your lawn, driveway, trees and landscaping stay where they are.

There are two main ways to do it. Pipe lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. Pipe bursting pulls a brand-new pipe through while breaking the old one apart. The camera tells us which one you need.

Services

Four ways we fix it, without the trench.

Every job starts with a CCTV camera inspection. We match the method to your pipe, not the other way around.

CIPP · pipe in a pipe

Sewer pipe lining

A resin liner cures inside your old pipe, creating a seamless, jointless pipe within a pipe. No trench, and no joints for roots to find.

Best forCracksLeaksRoot intrusion
HDPE · full replacement

Pipe bursting

A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe is pulled in behind it. A completely new pipe with a 50+ year lifespan, and it can be upsized for better flow.

Best forCollapsed linesOrangeburgClayUpsizing
CTS poly · pressure tested

Water line replacement

We pull a new CTS poly line underground, reconnect it, and test it for pressure and leaks. No rust or scale buildup, and better water pressure.

Best forOld galvanizedLeaksLow pressure
Spot repair · localized relining

Point repairs

When the damage is isolated to one section and the rest of the pipe is sound, we reline just that spot instead of replacing the whole line.

How much does trenchless sewer repair cost in Spokane?

It depends on the length of the line, how deep it is, how easy it is to reach, and whether it needs lining or bursting. After a camera inspection you get a written quote with a fixed scope, so there are no surprises.

Honest price range from Mike.

Is a lined pipe as strong as a new one?

A cured-in-place liner forms a seamless, jointless pipe inside the old one. With no joints, roots have nowhere to get in. Design life and liner spec.

Will you have to dig at all?

Usually one or two small access points, not a trench. If the camera shows a sag or a section that's out of grade, digging that one spot may be the better fix, and we'll tell you before we start.

How long does the job take, and can I use my plumbing?

Most jobs take X day(s). Your water or sewer is off for hours, not days, and we'll tell you exactly when.

Can you fix a pipe full of tree roots?

Yes. We clear the roots, then line the pipe so there are no joints for them to grow back through.

What if my pipe has collapsed?

Lining needs a pipe that still holds its shape. For a collapsed or crushed line, pipe bursting pulls a brand-new HDPE pipe through the old path.

Do you work on commercial and industrial properties?

Yes. Along with homes, we repair and replace lines for commercial and industrial properties, and we choose methods that keep your property running during the work.

How do you decide which method to use?

We look at the pipe on camera, plus access points, soil, surface use and nearby utilities. Then we recommend the method that fits: bursting for full replacement, lining for sound host pipes, or a point repair when damage is isolated. You get the reasoning, schedule and budget in writing.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewer line repair?

It depends on your policy and any service-line coverage you carry. We can provide camera footage and a written report for your insurer. Mike to add what he typically sees.
