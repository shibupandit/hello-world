Services

Four ways we fix it, without the trench.

Every job starts with a CCTV camera inspection. We match the method to your pipe, not the other way around.

CIPP · pipe within a pipe

Sewer pipe lining

A resin liner cures inside your old pipe, creating a seamless, jointless pipe within a pipe. No trench, and no joints for roots to find.

CracksLeaksRoot intrusion
HDPE · full replacement

Pipe bursting

A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe is pulled in behind it. A completely new pipe with a 50+ year lifespan, and it can be upsized for better flow.

Collapsed linesOrangeburgClayUpsizing
CTS poly · pressure tested

Water line pulling

We pull a new CTS poly line underground, reconnect it, and test it for pressure and leaks. No rust or scale buildup, and better water pressure.

Old galvanizedLeaksLow pressure
Spot repair · localized relining

Point repairs

When the damage is isolated to one section and the rest of the pipe is sound, we reline just that spot instead of replacing the whole line.

Why trenchless

The repair price is only part of the cost.

With a traditional dig you also pay to restore everything that came out of the ground. Trenchless keeps it in place.

How much does trenchless sewer repair cost?

It depends on the length and depth of the line, how easy it is to reach, and the method. After a camera inspection you get a written quote with a fixed scope, so there are no surprises. Typical price range.

Draft · confirm with Mike
How do you decide which method to use?

We look at the pipe on camera, plus access points, soil, surface use and nearby utilities. Then we recommend the method that fits: bursting for full replacement, lining for sound host pipes, or a point repair when damage is isolated. You get the reasoning, schedule and budget in writing.

Will you have to dig at all?

Usually one or two small access points, not a trench. If the camera shows a sag or a section that's out of grade, digging that one spot may be the better fix, and we'll tell you before we start.

Do you work on commercial and industrial properties?

Yes. Along with homes, we repair and replace lines for commercial and industrial properties, and we choose methods that keep your property running during the work.

How long does a pipe last after bursting or lining?

New HDPE pipe from bursting has a 50+ year lifespan. Lined pipe is seamless and resists leaks, roots and corrosion. Liner design life.

Can you fix a pipe full of tree roots?

Yes. We clear the roots, then line or replace the pipe so there are no joints for them to grow back through.
