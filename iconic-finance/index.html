How it works

Four steps from conversation to settlement

You'll know exactly what happens next at every stage — and what we need from you.

  1. Borrowing power assessment

    A structured conversation about your goals, income, deposit and debts. No cost, no obligation, no credit check at this stage.

  2. Lender comparison

    We match your situation against lender policy, structure, rates and fees — and show you why we recommend what we recommend.

  3. Application & approval

    We prepare the application, manage documents and handle lender communication. You stay informed at every step.

  4. Settlement & beyond

    We coordinate the final stages, then enrol you in our annual review program — your rate benchmarked, your structure checked and any fixed-rate expiry monitored, every year.

Self-employed across two entities

Situation
Company director with income split between two businesses and significant add-backs.
Challenge

Outcome

Refinance to fix the structure

Family with an owner-occupier loan and an investment loan cross-collateralised at one bank.

Outcome
Loans separated across lenders; repayments reduced and equity released for the next purchase.
