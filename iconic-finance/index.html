Real situations, properly structured

Anonymised case studies from actual client files — shared with permission.

First home with a 10% deposit

Situation
Couple in their late 20s, combined PAYG income, saving toward an apartment.
Challenge
LMI cost at 90% LVR was eroding their deposit strategy.
Outcome
Profession-based LMI waiver identified with a suitable lender; purchased within budget.

Self-employed across two entities

Situation
Company director with income split between two businesses and significant add-backs.
Challenge
Their bank assessed only the salary drawn, cutting borrowing capacity sharply.
Outcome
Application restructured around full financials with a lender whose policy recognised the true income position.

Refinance to fix the structure

Situation
Family with an owner-occupier loan and an investment loan cross-collateralised at one bank.
Challenge
The structure limited equity access and future purchase options.
Outcome
Loans separated across lenders; repayments reduced and equity released for the next purchase.

Outcomes depend on individual circumstances and lender policy. Past results don't guarantee future outcomes.
