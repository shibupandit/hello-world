Lenders we work with

Access to a wide panel matters less than knowing which lender's policy fits your situation. That's the part we do before any application is lodged.

How we recommend a lender

Major banks

Broad product ranges, strong offset and package offerings, and competitive pricing at lower LVRs.

Second-tier and regional lenders

Often sharper on price and more flexible on income types, particularly for self-employed and variable-income borrowers.

Non-bank lenders

Useful where a bank's policy doesn't fit: alternative documentation, credit history nuances, or unusual security types.

Specialist and construction funders

Construction, bridging, and complex or short-term scenarios where mainstream lenders won't participate.

Lender panel access is provided through our aggregator and changes over time. Not every lender on the panel will suit every borrower or scenario.

Before you get in touch

Researching first? Try our borrowing power calculator

What does a mortgage broker cost?

For standard residential lending our service costs you nothing. We receive a commission from the lender you settle with, disclosed in your Credit Guide and credit proposal before you commit to anything.

Is the first conversation an obligation-free assessment?

Yes. There's no catch and no obligation. The first conversation is how we both work out whether we're the right fit. Booking a chat or an assessment involves no credit check.

How long does approval usually take?

Lodgement to approval commonly runs days to a few weeks, depending on the lender and complexity. You get proactive updates at every milestone: lodged, valuation done, conditionally approved, unconditional.

Can you help if I'm self-employed or my income is complex?

Complicated is the specialty: self-employed income, equity compensation, multiple entities, portfolio structures. See self-employed home loans.

What documents will I need?

Nothing is required for the first conversation. A rough idea of your income, debts and savings makes it more useful. Document collection comes later, once, through our secure portal, with a checklist tailored to you.

How do you choose which lenders to recommend?

We are legally required to act in your best interests. In practice that means comparing options on structure, policy fit, rate and fees, and giving you a written recommendation that shows the reasoning.
