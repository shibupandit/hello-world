An injury can take away your income, your mobility and your plans in a single moment. Insurance companies know this, and they move fast to limit what they pay. Our job is to move faster.

Hartwell & Crane has represented injured Texans for more than three decades. A partner leads every case. We investigate before evidence disappears, hire the experts the case needs, and prepare every file as if it will be tried in front of a jury. That preparation is what produces fair settlements.

Personal injury cases are handled on contingency. You pay no attorney fee unless we recover money for you.

Cases we handle

  • Truck accidents

    18-wheelers, delivery vans and commercial fleets, including hours-of-service violations.

  • Car accidents

    Rear-end, intersection and highway collisions, including uninsured drivers.

  • Motorcycle accidents

    Riders face bias from insurers. We counter it with reconstruction experts.

  • Wrongful death

    Claims for spouses, children and parents who lost a family member.

  • Premises liability

    Falls, inadequate security and dangerous conditions on someone else's property.

  • Brain and spinal injuries

    Catastrophic injuries that need lifetime care plans and economic experts.

  • Product liability

    Defective vehicles, tires, machinery and consumer products.

  • Dog bites

    Claims against owners who knew, or should have known, a dog was dangerous.

What you can recover

Economic damages

  • Past and future medical expenses
  • Lost wages and loss of earning capacity
  • Home modifications and long-term care
  • Property damage

Non-economic damages

  • Physical pain and mental anguish
  • Physical impairment
  • Disfigurement
  • Loss of companionship (wrongful death)

Texas law

Exemplary (punitive) damages require clear and convincing evidence of fraud, malice or gross negligence. They are capped at the greater of $200,000 or twice the economic damages plus non-economic damages up to $750,000 (Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 41.008).

How your case moves

  1. Week 1

    Free consultation and preservation letters

    A partner reviews your case. If we take it, we send preservation letters to every party that holds evidence.

  2. Months 1–3

    Investigation and medical treatment

    We gather records, interview witnesses and retain experts while you focus on recovery.

  3. Months 3–6

    Demand and negotiation

    Once your treatment is far enough along to value the claim, we present a documented demand to the insurer.

  4. Months 6–18

    Lawsuit, discovery and trial

    If the offer is not fair, we file suit. Most cases settle during discovery. The rest we try.

Personal injury questions

How much is my case worth?

It depends on the severity of the injury, the medical costs now and in the future, lost income, and the insurance available. We can give you a realistic range once we have your medical records.

How long do I have to file?

In Texas, most injury and wrongful death suits must be filed within two years. Claims against a government entity require written notice much sooner, often within six months.

What if I was partly at fault?

You can still recover as long as you are not more than 50 percent responsible. Your recovery is reduced by your share of fault.

Will my case go to trial?

Most cases settle. Insurers settle fairly when they know the lawyer on the other side is ready to try the case, which is why we prepare every case for trial.
