Hartwell & Crane wins $14.2 million verdict against national trucking carrier

A Dallas County jury found the carrier liable after evidence showed the driver’s logbook had been altered.

Published
Nov 3, 2026
Location
Dallas, Texas
Release
For immediate release

DALLAS, Texas, November 3, 2026. A Dallas County jury has returned a $14.2 million verdict in favor of a North Texas family whose father was seriously injured when a tractor-trailer crossed the center line on Interstate 20.

The jury found the national trucking carrier liable after Hartwell & Crane presented evidence that the driver’s electronic logging records had been edited to hide more than four hours of driving beyond federal limits.

“This family did everything right. The evidence showed the carrier knew its driver was exhausted and kept him on the road anyway. The jury held them to account.”

Eleanor Hartwell, Founding Partner

About the case

  • Court: 162nd District Court, Dallas County
  • Trial length: 9 days
  • Trial team: Eleanor Hartwell, Daniel Reyes and Rosa Delgado

The carrier has 30 days to file post-trial motions. The family has asked for privacy and will not give interviews.
