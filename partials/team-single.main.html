Biography

Eleanor Hartwell has spent more than three decades in Texas courtrooms, first as a prosecutor and, since 1994, as a trial lawyer for injured people and their families.

She co-founded Hartwell & Crane after eight years with the Dallas County District Attorney's Office, where she tried more than 90 felony cases to verdict. That experience shaped the firm's approach: prepare every case as if a jury will decide it.

Eleanor leads the firm's catastrophic injury and wrongful death practice. She has obtained more than 40 verdicts and settlements over $1 million, including record recoveries against commercial trucking companies and product manufacturers.

Outside the courtroom, she teaches trial advocacy at SMU Dedman School of Law and chairs the firm's pro bono committee.
