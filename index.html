Common Questions

Before you call

Straight answers to what clients ask us most. For anything else, a short call is free.

Ask a Question
How much does a consultation cost?

Nothing. Your first consultation is free and confidential, whether by phone, video or in our Dallas office.

How do contingency fees work?

In injury and most employment cases we are paid a percentage of what we recover. If there is no recovery, you owe no attorney fee.

How long will my case take?

Most matters resolve in 9 to 18 months. Cases that go to trial can take longer. We give you a realistic timeline at the first meeting and update it as the case develops.

Will I work with a partner or an associate?

A partner leads every case and is your main contact. Associates and paralegals support the work so it moves quickly and costs less.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Any documents you have: police or incident reports, medical records, letters, contracts, photos and insurance details. If you have nothing yet, come anyway.
