Is my first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first consultation is free and confidential, whether by phone, video or in one of our offices.

How quickly will someone respond?

Within one business day for online requests. Calls to our 24/7 line are answered by a person at any hour.

What should I have ready?

Dates, names of the people or companies involved, and any documents you already have: police reports, letters, contracts or photos. Don't worry if you have none of these yet.

Can we meet outside office hours?

Yes. Evening, weekend, hospital and home visits are available by appointment for clients who can't come to us.

Do you handle cases outside these four cities?

Yes. We represent clients across Texas, including Plano and Arlington, and meet by video when travel is difficult.
