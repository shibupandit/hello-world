Our Story

Built in the courtroom, not the boardroom.

In 1994, Eleanor Hartwell and Marcus Crane left the Dallas County District Attorney's Office with a shared frustration: too many people were facing the most important moment of their lives with a lawyer who had never seen the inside of a courtroom.

They opened a two-room office on Commerce Street and built the firm around trial work. Every case was prepared as if it would be decided by a jury, because insurers and prosecutors offer fair outcomes to lawyers they know will go the distance.

The firm has grown to 14 attorneys and four offices, but the model has not changed. We keep a deliberately limited docket, a named partner leads every matter, and clients have a direct line to the lawyer handling their case.

  • A partner on every case. Your matter is led by a named partner, not handed down.
  • Trial-ready from day one. We hire experts and build the record early.
  • Straight answers. You will always know where your case stands and what it costs.

What We Stand For

Four commitments we make to every client.

They are written into how we staff, prepare and bill every case, not just printed on a wall.

  • Preparation wins

    We investigate early, hire the right experts and build every file to be tried in front of a jury.

  • Honest advice

    If your case is weak, we tell you. If we can't help, we will refer you to someone who can.

  • Direct access

    You get your attorney's direct number, and calls are returned the same business day.

  • Clear fees

    Contingency, flat or hourly, agreed in writing before work begins. No surprise invoices.
