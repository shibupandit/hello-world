Jury Verdict · Personal Injury

Trucking collision leaves father with spinal injuries

A commercial carrier was found negligent after evidence showed its driver had been on duty for nineteen hours.

$18.4M Jury verdict
Practice area
Personal Injury
Case type
Trucking collision
Court
Dallas County District Court
Year
2025

The challenge

Our client, a 46-year-old electrician, was stopped at a red light on Interstate 30 when a loaded tractor-trailer struck his pickup from behind. He suffered two fractured vertebrae and has not been able to return to work.

The carrier’s insurer offered $750,000 within weeks of the crash and argued our client had stopped suddenly.

Our approach

  • We sent a preservation letter within 48 hours, securing the truck’s engine data and the driver’s electronic logs.
  • The logs showed the driver had been on duty for nineteen hours, far beyond federal limits.
  • A reconstruction expert showed the truck never braked before impact.
  • A life-care planner and economist documented the full cost of our client’s injuries.

The outcome

After a two-week trial, the Dallas County jury returned a verdict of $18.4 million, including $4.1 million in punitive damages against the carrier for knowingly scheduling an exhausted driver.

“They never treated me like a file number. Eleanor was in that courtroom every day, and she made sure the jury understood what my family lost.”

Client, Dallas
